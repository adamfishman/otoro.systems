From August 2023 to October 2024 I worked with Signet Jewelers as a senior Java developer, on a remote team of on-shore and off-shore engineers.

## In-store repair tracking

Signet tracks jewelry repairs in its stores with an internal system. I modernized its legacy codebase with Spring Boot.

## A custom jewelry design wizard

I helped architect a wizard for designing custom jewelry. My focus was the data model underneath it: the matrix of metals, sizes, shapes and stones that every design choice depends on.
