CrashBay runs a marketplace for collision repair, used by customers, insurance clients and claims adjusters. I've worked with the team since March 2025, on both the platform they had and the one they're building next.

## Moving the legacy app

The original product was an ASP.NET app built on the Umbraco CMS, running on third-party hosting. I migrated it to Azure infrastructure the company owns. It's more reliable now, and it costs less to run.

## A claims integration for launch

Insurance clients needed to send repair assignments from day one. I built a two-way integration with Snapsheet Vice, a claims management platform, on Cloudflare Workers. Those clients could send assignments at launch.

## Rebuilding the platform

I'm co-leading a ground-up rebuild in Ruby on Rails. It includes a network shop locator for customers and claims adjusters.

## Research that sets direction

Part of the work is deciding what to build next. My technical research guides the roadmap, including the recommendation to fully replace a six-year-old MVP.
